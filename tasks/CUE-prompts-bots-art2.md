# Ten character pictures for a billiards game

Draw the ten characters below, one picture each, **in exactly the same art style as the attached picture** (same painting
technique, colours, light and face style - only the character changes).

Rules for every picture:
- Waist-up. The head near the top, the face in the middle left-to-right, the face about the same size as in the attached
  picture.
- Transparent background: only the person, nothing behind them. The lower edge, at the waist, fades into loose brush strokes.
- Holding a billiard cue as described. Fully clothed, modest, nothing suggestive. Every character is an adult.
- No text, no logo, no watermark, no frame.
- Square, 1024 × 1024, PNG with transparency.
- Each character must look clearly different from the others - face shape, skin, eyes, and above all the hair colour and
  style - exactly as described.
- Mouth closed: a closed-lip smile or a calm closed mouth, never an open-mouthed grin or laugh.
- Each character's country must show clearly and respectfully: the face and colouring of their people as described, and
  the clothes, jewellery and details of their country. Never a caricature - each one handsome or beautiful in their own way.

When all ten are done: name each file exactly as its ID below (for example `bot-daeun.png`) and put all ten in one zip
file named `CUE-batch-bots2.zip`.

## The characters

**bot-daeun** — Korea. Korean woman, 21, a freshman in a university billiards club. A Korean face: fair warm skin, a soft
oval face, gentle dark eyes with a slight inner double eyelid, a small nose, a sweet closed-lip smile. Glossy black
chin-length bob with a vivid cobalt-blue inner layer showing underneath, a small silver ear cuff. A navy Korean university
department jacket (gwajam) with leather sleeves and embroidered patches without readable letters, over a white hoodie; a
small red-and-blue norigae tassel charm hanging from the zip. Hugging the upright cue with both arms, her cheek leaning on
it, eager and a little nervous.

**bot-tane** — New Zealand. Māori man, 27, a rugby forward. A Polynesian face: warm brown skin, a broad strong face, a
wide nose, deep dark eyes, a short black beard, a confident closed-mouth smile; big and broad-shouldered. Thick black
curly hair tied up in a top knot. A plain black rugby jersey with a white collar (no logo), a carved green pounamu
(greenstone) koru pendant on a cord at his neck. Holding the cue across his chest in both hands like a rugby ball about
to be passed, standing square and strong.

**bot-luiza** — Brazil. Afro-Brazilian woman, 26, a samba teacher from Rio. A Brazilian face: warm deep-brown skin, full
cheeks, big bright dark eyes, a joyful closed-lip smile. Big voluminous natural curls with golden-honey ends, a small
cluster of green and gold carnival feathers clipped at one side. A green-and-yellow track jacket zipped up over a white top,
big gold hoop earrings, colourful beaded bracelets. Caught mid-samba step, holding the cue level in both hands at chest
height, curls swinging.

**bot-amara** — Nigeria. Yoruba woman, 26, a fashion designer from Lagos. A West African face: deep dark-brown skin with a
warm glow, high cheekbones, large dark eyes with long lashes, full lips in a proud closed-lip smile. A tall sculpted gele
headwrap in orange-and-teal ankara print, long black braids falling from under it. A tailored jacket of the same bold
ankara print, a necklace of red coral beads, gold earrings. One hand on her hip, the cue held upright in the other, chin
up like on a runway.

**bot-darya** — Russia. Russian woman, 28, a Russian pyramid billiards champion from Moscow. A Slavic face: pale fair skin,
high cheekbones, a straight nose, cool grey eyes under straight brows, a composed closed-lip smile. Dark chestnut hair in a
thick braid wound around her head like a crown. A long black coat with a dark fur collar, and over her shoulders a Russian
Pavlovo Posad shawl - black with large red and gold roses - a small silver brooch. Standing straight, the coat swept back,
the cue held firmly at an angle across her body, looking straight at the viewer.

**bot-priya** — India. Indian woman, 29, a software engineer from Bengaluru. A South Indian face: warm medium-brown skin,
large dark eyes lined with kajal, thick dark eyebrows, a small gold nose stud, a small red bindi, a thoughtful closed-lip
smile. Thick long black hair in one side braid with a string of white jasmine flowers (gajra) woven in. A deep-teal kurta
with gold embroidery and a soft maroon dupatta over one shoulder, gold jhumka earrings, thin gold bangles. The cue tucked
under one arm, tapping her chin with a chalk cube, looking up and to the side as if solving a puzzle.

**bot-jose** — Philippines. Filipino man, 38, a street-pool legend from Manila. A Filipino face: warm tan-brown skin, a
broad friendly face, kind dark eyes with laugh lines, a short moustache, an easy closed-mouth smile. Short black hair, a
little spiky on top. A cream barong-style shirt of thin fabric with fine embroidery down the front, sleeves rolled, a
woven bracelet. Leaning on the upright cue with one elbow, flipping a chalk cube in his other hand like a magician.

**bot-ole** — Norway. Norwegian man, 46, a fisherman from Bergen. A Nordic face: weathered fair skin, ruddy cheeks, pale
blue eyes with sun crinkles, a strong nose, a calm closed mouth. Long copper-red hair tied back, a full copper-red beard. A
traditional Norwegian Selbu knitted sweater - black and white with the eight-pointed star pattern - and pewter buttons.
Standing with the cue planted upright beside him like a ship's mast, one hand high on it, gazing calmly into the distance.

**bot-frederic** — Belgium. Belgian man, 52, a three-cushion grand master. A Western European face: light skin, a long
refined face, deep-set calm grey eyes, a neatly trimmed greying moustache, an unreadable closed mouth. Dark hair greying at
the temples, combed back. Tournament dress: crisp white shirt, black waistcoat, black bow tie, a white billiard glove on
his bridge hand, a tiny black-yellow-red enamel pin on the waistcoat. Holding the cue upright in front of him with both
hands like a sword at rest, chin slightly raised.

**bot-elijah** — USA. African-American man, 34, a wandering pool prodigy from Kentucky - laid-back, cocky, a genius who
plays fast. A face of his people: rich dark-brown skin, a strong jaw, a thin moustache, sharp amused eyes, a lopsided
closed-mouth smirk. Short hair under a black flat-brimmed cowboy hat. A worn brown leather duster coat over a dark shirt,
a western bolo tie with a turquoise stone, a leather wristband. Slouched easily against the cue held loosely at his side,
one thumb hooked in his belt, looking at the viewer as if he already knows how the game ends.
