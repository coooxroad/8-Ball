# CUE image prompts — 3D props for the main menu cards (owner 11-20)

Seven pictures, one per menu card, shown large in the middle of each card in place of its line icon (the cards are
printed card stock in a billiard colour each: blue, green, red, amber, purple, slate, olive). Send them back named exactly
as the IDs below (PNG with transparency), or one zip `CUE-batch-menu-props.zip`. Claude makes them 512 × 512 WebP
`web/ui/menu-<id>.webp`; they show when 디버그 · 메인 카드 그림 is set to 3D 소품.

## COMMON RULES (all seven)

- 1024 × 1024, **transparent background**. One object (or one small group) centred, filling about 80% of the width, nothing
  touching the edges. A soft, semi-transparent contact shadow under it (part of the PNG, falling to the lower left).
- A real product photograph, as it would look in a good store catalogue today: real materials, real proportions, a
  little honest wear. Not a toy, not cartoon, not cute, not "AI art": no heavy gloss, no perfect symmetry, no ornaments,
  no gold leaf, no antique look, no glow effects unless the prop says so.
- Seen from the front, a little from above (about 20°), turned slightly (three-quarter view). **Light from the upper
  right**, soft, one light. All seven lit and shot the same way, so they look like one set.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos or brand marks anywhere (balls are plain colours, with no number).
- Colours rich but natural; each prop must stand out on its card's colour (given below).
- For players of every age: calm, solid, grown-up objects.
